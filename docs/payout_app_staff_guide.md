# Drive N Style Payouts – staff guide

This app reads the day's invoice PDFs, works out the labour charge and the
spot incentive, and keeps the record of what was paid. The amounts are
calculated by the app; you record the payments.

## First time on a PC

1. Open **Drive N Style Payouts** from the desktop shortcut.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then
   **Run anyway**. This is asked only once.
2. Go to **Set-up** and press **Sign in**. Microsoft Edge opens: sign in
   with your own work Google account and press **Allow**.
   If Google says the app is not verified, click **Advanced**, then
   **Go to Drive N Style Payouts**.
3. Under **Invoices**, press **Browse…** and choose the folder where you
   save the invoice PDFs.

## Every day

1. In Zoho, save the new invoices as PDF into the invoice folder.
2. Open the app, go to **Scan** and press **Scan now**.
   The tiles show how many invoices were posted and how many need review.
3. If **Review** shows a number, open it. Each line is a name the app does
   not know (a salesperson, a vehicle or an item).
   - If it is only written differently, choose the right name and press
     **Save match and scan again**.
   - If it is really new, tell the owner so it can be added to the masters.
   - If the invoice should not be paid on, choose it under **Or cancel
     invoice**, type the reason and press **Cancel invoice**.
4. Go to **Payouts**. The list shows what is waiting to be paid.
   **Payout slip…** shows the total for each person.
5. After paying, tick the lines you paid and press **Record payment…**.
   Enter the date, how it was paid, the reference number, and choose the
   screenshot or photo as proof. One payment can cover many lines.

## Rules to remember

- A payment needs a **reference number or a proof**. Give both when you can.
- **Do not type in the Google Sheet.** Amounts changed there by hand are
  put back by the app and written in the log with your name.
- Paid the wrong line? Tick it, press **Reopen…**, type the reason, and
  record it again correctly.
- A payment that must wait: tick it and press **Hold…** with the reason.
  **Release** brings it back.
- An invoice edited in Zoho: save its PDF again in the same folder. The
  app corrects unpaid lines and adds an adjustment line for paid ones.

## Once a week: sign in again

Google signs the app out every 7 days. When a scan says you are not signed
in, go to **Set-up**, press **Sign in** and allow it again. Nothing is lost.

## If something goes wrong

- **A message says the masters sheet has a problem** – nothing is
  calculated until the owner corrects the row named in the message.
- **"The sheet was not shared" / "not allowed to open"** – ask the owner to
  share the sheet with your Google account.
- **An unexpected error** – the app saves the details in a file and shows
  where. Send that file to the owner.
- **No internet** – the app cannot post or record. Try again when it is back.

**History** shows everything done through the app, with who and when.
